# Founder Evaluation

## Purpose
Assess whether the founders can execute the roadmap — and how they respond to hard questioning, which is itself a signal.

## Inputs
- Founder background, team composition, roles, prior experience.
- The founder's behavior during the simulated meeting (evasive vs specific answers).

## Assessment dimensions
- Domain experience and role-fit (who does what).
- Engineering capability for the roadmap (seniority, headcount).
- Prior execution evidence (shipped products, exits, scaling).
- Ability to answer hard questions with evidence rather than deflection.

## Decision rules
- A founder who repeatedly avoids questions is a risk signal, recorded in the transcript and findings.
- Team gaps are findings, not assumptions — be specific about what is missing.
- Do not praise a founder for a convincing pitch; a good pitch is not execution evidence.

## Outputs
`diligence_findings` (category `team`) and transcript analysis of founder responses.

## Limitations
- No background checks or references; founder claims are founder-reported.
- Can only assess what is stated or demonstrated in the meeting.

## Evaluation criteria
- Team composition and role-fit are explicit.
- Founders are not automatically praised.
- Evasion patterns are surfaced as findings.
